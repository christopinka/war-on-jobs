# Expat Remote SaaS Strategy

## Goal
Create a realistic path to work for remote-friendly SaaS companies while living in Spain or Portugal, using a structure that supports low student loan payments and a lower-cost lifestyle.

## Core idea
Target companies that are:
- remote-first or remote-friendly
- comfortable with international hiring
- likely to use EOR or payroll partners
- aligned with backend, platform, integration, modernization, and enterprise systems work

## Why this fits your background
Your background is especially relevant for:
- backend engineering
- platform and developer productivity work
- integrations and middleware
- cloud migration and modernization
- enterprise systems and regulated environments
- reliability and operational tooling

## Best target company types
- SaaS companies with global teams
- remote-first software companies
- companies already hiring internationally
- companies using Deel, Oyster, Remote, or similar EOR structures
- companies in fintech, healthtech, infrastructure, developer tools, or enterprise software

## Best job titles to target
- Backend Engineer
- Platform Engineer
- Integration Engineer
- Software Engineer
- Developer Experience Engineer
- DevOps Engineer
- SRE
- Solutions Engineer
- Systems Engineer
- Staff Engineer
- Technical Architect

## What to look for in job postings
Search for terms like:
- remote
- distributed team
- async
- global team
- international remote
- EOR
- contractor / global payroll
- work from anywhere
- timezone flexibility
- remote-first

## Financial logic
The strategy works best when the overall setup is intentional:
- keep income at a level that supports the FEIE strategy
- maintain IDR enrollment and annual recertification
- avoid unnecessary income spikes that undermine the structure
- keep a low-cost lifestyle and plan for healthcare and residency needs

## Key risk areas
- companies that only hire within the U.S.
- companies without international payroll support
- roles with high compensation that destroy the low-income structure
- unclear legal or tax setup
- poor residency or healthcare planning

## Recommended next steps
1. Build a list of 25–40 remote-friendly SaaS companies.
2. Prioritize those with global hiring or international payroll support.
3. Search for roles that fit your actual strengths.
4. Tailor applications around backend/platform/integration/modernization work.
5. Keep the student loan structure and tax planning in view as part of the decision-making process.
6. Keep the lifestyle plan in mind: Spain or Portugal should be treated as an intentional relocation strategy, not a passive dream.
