# FEIE Countries and Notes

## Framing
- FEIE is primarily relevant for U.S. citizens living abroad who qualify under the foreign earned income exclusion rules.
- The practical value depends on whether the person is genuinely living abroad, meeting the physical presence or bona fide residence test, and keeping income in the right range for the structure to remain useful.

## Notes on country selection
- Some countries are attractive because they have lower cost of living and more accessible expatriate lifestyles.
- Some are attractive because they are easier to integrate into a remote-work or international-hiring setup.
- Some are attractive because they fit a longer-term relocation strategy rather than short-term travel.

## Practical filters for choosing a country
- Visa and residency practicality
- Healthcare access and quality
- Cost of living
- Internet / remote-work infrastructure
- Language and cultural fit
- Tax and legal complexity
- Ease of staying compliant with U.S. tax obligations

## Short version
- Spain and Portugal are strong candidates for a more structured lifestyle and remote-work plan.
- Mexico, Costa Rica, Panama, and parts of Latin America can be attractive for affordability and practicality.
- Some Asian and Middle Eastern locations can be attractive for cost and lifestyle, but they require more care around legal and residency planning.
