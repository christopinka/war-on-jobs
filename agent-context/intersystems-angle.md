# InterSystems Angle

## Why InterSystems is interesting
- InterSystems is a long-standing healthcare software company with a strong reputation in clinical and health information systems.
- It sits in the part of the healthcare software world that is less flashy than the big consumer-facing platforms, but often more mission-critical and technically interesting.
- The company has a reputation for building systems that are deeply integrated with hospital operations and healthcare data workflows.

## Why it may fit your background
- Strong fit for backend, integration, platform, interoperability, and data-heavy engineering work.
- Good fit if you like systems that are operationally important and tied to real healthcare workflows rather than purely product-led or marketing-led environments.
- The work can be especially relevant for people interested in interoperability, data exchange, reliability, and enterprise health systems.

## What to research
- Current hiring priorities and engineering teams
- Whether there are roles in backend, integration, platform, data, or developer tooling
- Whether the company is hiring globally or primarily in specific regions
- Whether the work is more focused on healthcare operations, interoperability, or platform infrastructure

## Good role angles to target
- Backend Engineer
- Integration Engineer
- Platform Engineer
- Data Engineer
- Software Engineer
- Systems Engineer
- Interoperability / Healthcare data engineering roles

## Strategic framing
- InterSystems is a credible target if the goal is healthcare software with real technical depth and operational seriousness.
- It is especially worth pursuing if you want to lean into healthcare data, integration, and systems work rather than general SaaS or consumer product work.
