# Established SaaS Companies and Healthcare Globally

## Framing
- The established SaaS angle is different from the cloud-platform angle.
- These companies are more relevant when the target is healthcare workflow software, customer/member engagement, operations, workforce systems, or enterprise platforms rather than pure infrastructure.

## Strongest fits

### Salesforce
- One of the clearest established SaaS players in healthcare.
- Strong in patient and member engagement, CRM, Health Cloud, and AI-assisted workflows.
- Good fit if the work is around platforms, integrations, data models, workflow automation, or customer-facing healthcare products.

### ServiceNow
- Strong in workflow automation, case management, IT service management, and operational tooling for healthcare organizations.
- Especially relevant for organizations that want better internal operations, service workflows, and digital process modernization.
- Good fit for backend, platform, workflow, and integration-oriented roles.

### Workday
- Strong in workforce, HR, finance, and planning systems for healthcare organizations.
- More operational and enterprise-software oriented than product-engineering oriented, but still relevant globally.
- Better fit for people who like enterprise systems and business workflow software.

### SAP
- Strong in global enterprise software, ERP, supply chain, and large-scale operations.
- Relevant in healthcare organizations that need complex back-office and operational systems.
- Good if the target is enterprise systems, integration, and large-scale platform work.

### Epic
- A major healthcare software vendor with a large installed base.
- More healthcare-specific and implementation-heavy than general SaaS, but still highly relevant if the target is healthcare software broadly.
- Best viewed as a healthcare-software company rather than a general SaaS company.

## Lower-priority but still relevant
- Adobe: more patient-experience and digital engagement than core platform work.
- Atlassian: more collaboration and workflow enablement than healthcare-specific product work.
- Other vertical SaaS vendors: relevant, but usually narrower in scope or more regionally concentrated.

## Best-fit role angles for this lane
- Backend Engineer
- Platform Engineer
- Integration Engineer
- Workflow / Business Automation Engineer
- Enterprise Software Engineer
- Data / Platform Engineer
- Product Engineer for healthcare workflows

## Global angle
- For global healthcare SaaS work, Salesforce and ServiceNow are probably the strongest broad targets.
- Workday and SAP are strong if the interest is more enterprise and operations-oriented.
- Epic is relevant, but it is more healthcare-software specific and often more implementation-heavy.
