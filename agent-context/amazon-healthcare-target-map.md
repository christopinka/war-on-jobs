# Amazon Healthcare Target Map

## What Amazon is doing in healthcare
- Amazon’s healthcare strategy is broad and fragmented rather than a single clear bet.
- The company is active in three main areas: care delivery, pharmacy/consumer health, and technology/infrastructure for healthcare.
- The most natural fit for Amazon’s strengths is pharmacy, logistics, and healthcare commerce rather than hospital-style operations.

## Main buckets to target
### 1. AWS / cloud / data / AI for healthcare
- Good fit if your background is in backend, platform, integration, data engineering, or cloud systems.
- Relevant themes: healthcare data, interoperability, workflow automation, analytics, AI tooling, and developer/platform work.
- This is likely the most promising lane if you want to stay close to software engineering rather than healthcare operations.

### 2. Amazon Pharmacy / consumer healthcare
- Focus on product, platform, logistics, and integration work.
- This is the clearest expression of Amazon’s core strengths applied to healthcare.
- Good fit for people who like distributed systems, reliability, operations, and customer-facing software.

### 3. One Medical / care delivery related work
- This is more operationally complex and less obviously aligned with pure software engineering.
- It can still be relevant, but it is less clean as a target if your preference is backend/platform/engineering work.

## Practical takeaway
- If the goal is software engineering leverage, prioritize AWS and healthcare-tech/product work over direct care-delivery roles.
- If the goal is to work inside Amazon’s healthcare ecosystem more broadly, keep pharmacy and consumer-health roles on the list as secondary targets.

## Best-fit angles for you
- Backend engineer
- Platform engineer
- Integration engineer
- Data / analytics / ML infrastructure
- Developer productivity / internal tooling
- Reliability / operational tooling
- Systems engineer

## What to research before applying
- Which Amazon orgs are actually hiring in healthcare-related software roles
- Whether the role is more about infrastructure, product, or operations
- Whether the job is remote-friendly or tied to a specific office/region
- The likely team maturity and engineering culture
