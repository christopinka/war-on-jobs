# 30-Day Job Search Checklist

## 1. Protect the current role
- Keep delivering well on the current contract.
- Make your wins visible to the team and stakeholders.
- Look for any path to extension, internal referral, or better alignment with the team.
- Keep the current role as a platform, not as the final destination.

## 2. Define your target profile
- Focus on roles in backend/platform engineering, migration, modernization, integration, and enterprise systems.
- Prioritize remote-first, async-friendly, and direct-hire or high-signal opportunities.
- Avoid broad applications that are not aligned with your background.

## 3. Build a target list
- Create a list of 20–30 target companies.
- Split them into:
  - direct-hire targets
  - contractor/consulting targets
  - warm-introduction targets
- Rank them by fit, interest, and likelihood of response.

## 4. Reach out to your network
- Contact former colleagues, friends, and professional contacts.
- Ask for advice, referrals, and insight into teams or openings.
- Focus on quality conversations, not mass outreach.

## 5. Tailor applications
- Customize each application to the specific role.
- Emphasize relevant experience in modernization, migration, cloud, backend, and enterprise systems.
- Keep your messaging honest and specific.

## 6. Track what matters
- Track company, role, source, date applied, and response status.
- Note which channels produce conversations or referrals.
- Double down on the channels that are working.

## 7. Prepare your story
- Be ready to explain your recent job history clearly.
- Frame your time in the market as selective, intentional, and grounded in skill-building.
- Keep your message calm, credible, and focused on fit.

## 8. Review weekly
- Review progress every week.
- Cut low-value efforts.
- Increase attention to the best opportunities.
- Keep momentum moving without spreading yourself too thin.

## 9. Keep the long-term goal in view
- Do not settle for weak containers just to get busy.
- Work toward a better structure, stronger team fit, and more durable opportunity.
- Use the current role to build leverage, not to stop the search.
