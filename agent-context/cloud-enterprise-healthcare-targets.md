# Cloud and Enterprise Healthcare Targets

## Overall framing
- The big cloud and enterprise players are mostly doing healthcare through data platforms, interoperability, AI, enterprise software, and modernization rather than through a single consumer-facing healthcare product.
- For software engineering targeting, the most relevant angle is usually backend, platform, integration, data, AI, and enterprise systems work rather than care-delivery operations.

## Priority players

### Google Cloud
- Strongest pure-cloud health-data play.
- Focus is on healthcare data interoperability and AI using standards such as FHIR, HL7v2, DICOM, and de-identification workflows.
- Good fit for backend, platform, data engineering, AI infrastructure, and integrations.
- Likely one of the most relevant options if the goal is to stay close to modern software engineering work.

### Microsoft
- Very active in Azure-based healthcare modernization, interoperability, and AI.
- More enterprise-focused than consumer-health focused.
- Good fit for platform engineering, cloud modernization, enterprise systems, and data/AI work.

### Oracle
- More of an enterprise healthcare software vendor than a cloud-native player.
- Oracle Health is built around clinical, financial, payer, and population-health systems.
- Good if the target is regulated enterprise software and complex healthcare operations, but less clean as a pure software engineering target.

### IBM
- More consulting, data/AI, and life-sciences transformation than a typical cloud platform story.
- Strong on regulated environments, modernization, and healthcare/life-sciences transformation.
- Useful if the interest is in data/AI and enterprise modernization, though often more consulting-heavy.

### Salesforce
- More focused on health operations, CRM, patient/member experience, and workflow tools than infrastructure.
- Less relevant if the real target is backend/platform engineering.

## Ranking for your purposes
1. Google Cloud
2. Microsoft
3. Oracle
4. IBM
5. Salesforce

## Best-fit role angles
- Backend Engineer
- Platform Engineer
- Integration Engineer
- Data Engineer
- AI / ML Infrastructure Engineer
- Software Engineer
- Enterprise Systems Engineer
- Developer Productivity / Internal Tooling Engineer

## What to research before applying
- Which specific orgs inside each company are hiring for healthcare-related software roles
- Whether the job is mainly infrastructure, product, data, or consulting
- Whether the role is remote-friendly or tied to a specific office/region
- The maturity and structure of the team, especially around engineering practices and delivery culture
