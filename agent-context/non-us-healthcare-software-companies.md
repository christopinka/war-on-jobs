# Non-U.S. Healthcare Software and EHR Companies

## Broad set of non-U.S. and internationally relevant vendors

### Major international healthcare IT vendors
- Dedalus — Italy
- InterSystems — United States, but strongly international; often treated as a global healthcare software vendor
- Tietoevry — Finland
- CompuGroup Medical (CGM) — Germany
- Siemens Healthineers — Germany
- Philips — Netherlands
- GE HealthCare — United States, but globally significant in clinical technology
- Meditech — United States, but widely used internationally
- Allscripts — United States, but internationally visible in some markets
- Cerner / Oracle Health — United States, but globally important in healthcare IT
- Epic — United States, but global influence through large health-system deployments

### Regional and country-specific vendors
- EMIS — United Kingdom
- TPP — United Kingdom
- System C — United Kingdom
- ChipSoft — Netherlands
- Cambio — Sweden
- Imatis — Sweden

## What these companies generally do
- Provide hospital information systems, clinical workflow software, and health record platforms
- Support electronic health record, patient administration, and operational workflows
- Offer care management, analytics, interoperability, and enterprise healthcare IT tools
- Often have strong footprints in Europe and other international healthcare markets

## Why they matter
- They represent a meaningful set of employers if the target is healthcare software outside the U.S.
- They are especially relevant for roles involving integrations, interoperability, patient workflows, data platforms, and enterprise healthcare systems

## Best-fit role angles
- Backend Engineer
- Platform Engineer
- Integration Engineer
- Data Engineer
- Healthcare Systems Engineer
- Product Engineer for clinical workflows
- Enterprise Software Engineer

## Biggest non-U.S. footprint by region

### Europe
- Dedalus — widely used in Italy and across Southern and Central Europe
- Tietoevry — used across Nordic and broader European healthcare organizations
- CompuGroup Medical (CGM) — widely used in Germany and across parts of Europe
- Siemens Healthineers — used in many European hospitals and health systems
- Philips — widely deployed in European hospitals and clinical environments
- EMIS — used heavily in the United Kingdom
- TPP — used heavily in the United Kingdom
- System C — used in the United Kingdom healthcare market
- ChipSoft — widely used in the Netherlands
- Cambio — used in Sweden and nearby Nordic markets
- Imatis — used in Sweden and other Nordic healthcare settings

### Africa
- InterSystems — used in healthcare systems across parts of Africa, especially where international health organizations and large public health systems are involved
- Philips — present in hospital and clinical technology deployments across Africa
- Siemens Healthineers — used in some African hospital and clinical environments
- Oracle Health / Cerner — used in some regional hospital and enterprise deployments

### South America
- InterSystems — used by healthcare organizations in several South American markets
- Philips — present in hospital and clinical technology deployments across the region
- Siemens Healthineers — used in hospital and imaging environments in parts of South America
- Oracle Health / Cerner — present in larger health-system and enterprise deployments

### Australia and New Zealand
- InterSystems — used in healthcare organizations across Australia and New Zealand
- Oracle Health / Cerner — used in larger hospital and enterprise environments in Australia and New Zealand
- Philips — present in clinical and hospital technology deployments
- Siemens Healthineers — used in hospital imaging and clinical technology settings

### Asia-Pacific and other international markets
- Siemens Healthineers — used across many Asia-Pacific hospital and clinical environments
- Philips — widely deployed in Asia-Pacific hospital systems and connected care settings
- InterSystems — used in international health systems and cross-border deployment contexts
- Oracle Health / Cerner — used in large healthcare organizations across Asia-Pacific and other global markets
- Epic — used by some large hospital systems in international settings, though less dominant than in North America

### Global / multinational
- InterSystems — used by healthcare organizations in many countries and regions worldwide
- Meditech — used internationally in many hospitals, though historically strongest in North America
- Allscripts — used in some international healthcare settings, especially where larger health-system deployments exist
- Cerner / Oracle Health — used by large health systems around the world, especially in complex enterprise environments
- Epic — used by large health systems across multiple countries, though still most strongly associated with North America

## Global angle
- The strongest global healthcare software targets are often those with real international deployment footprints, not just U.S.-centric vendors
- Many of the non-U.S. players are particularly strong in Europe and region-specific healthcare IT ecosystems
