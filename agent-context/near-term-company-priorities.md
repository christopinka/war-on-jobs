# Near-Term Company Priorities

## Main priority
- Try to turn the current contract into something longer-term if the team, work, and compensation are good enough.
- The year of endless job search was demoralizing, so the aim is to create stability rather than keep treating every opportunity as a temporary stop.

## Target companies to develop background on
- Avanade
  - Focus on FTE conversion paths and internal mobility.
  - Understand how the organization structures delivery, consulting, and bench-related work.
- Accenture
  - Preferable to Avanade, if the fit and path are strong.
  - Build background on delivery practices, client work, staffing patterns, and possible route to a more durable role.
- Amazon
  - Especially worth exploring for healthcare-related opportunities.
  - Research the relevant orgs, engineering culture, and how hiring works for nontraditional paths.

## Consulting-firm angle
- Consulting firms often have a bench and actively try to keep people employed, which can be useful if the current arrangement is stable and the work is decent.
- The strategic question is not only whether the company is attractive, but whether it can give you a stronger base for the next 12–24 months.

## Working assumption
- The goal is to build a more durable position, not just to keep chasing the next short-term contract.
- Use the current role as leverage while also developing enough knowledge on these firms to be ready if a stronger path opens up.
