# Signal-Based Screening Rubric

Use this as a quick filter before spending time on a role or company.

## Positive signals
- The role has clear scope, ownership, and success criteria.
- The posting sounds grounded in real engineering work: backend, platform, integrations, reliability, modernization, or developer productivity.
- The company shows signs of engineering maturity through public docs, blog posts, open source work, or thoughtful product and engineering content.
- The company is remote-first or remote-friendly and shows evidence of async or distributed-team practices.
- The company appears to hire internationally or uses EOR or payroll partners.
- The job description is specific enough that the work feels legible rather than vague or performative.

## Red flags
- The role is defined in abstract, promotional language with no clear scope.
- The company seems more interested in optics than delivery.
- The interview process appears overly long, opaque, or poorly structured.
- The company only hires in the U.S. or shows no sign of global hiring support.
- The work looks misaligned with your actual strengths or would create an unnecessary tax or income burden.
