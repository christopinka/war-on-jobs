# LinkedIn 10-Second Screen

## Purpose
This is a quick triage checklist for LinkedIn job results. The goal is to avoid clicking through a large number of fake, vendor-heavy, or weak-fit roles.

## Before you click
Only click a result if all of these are true:
- clear employer name
- clear job title
- matches your stack
- matches your target domain
- fits your seniority / experience range
- direct employer or company-led posting
- no staffing, vendor, MSP, VMS, or consultant framing
- not a vague, repeated, or copy-paste job description

## Reject immediately
Skip anything with any of these signals:
- recruiter
- agency
- staffing
- MSP
- VMS
- vendor
- C2C
- consulting
- contract
- offshore
- “our client”
- “resource”
- “bench”
- vague company identity
- generic hiring copy

## Keep only roles that are clearly strong fit
A role is worth a click only if it is:
- clearly a real company role
- obviously relevant to your experience
- aligned to your preferred stack
- in a target domain you actually want
- likely to be a real employer-led hire

## Rule for time
If a role is not obviously good in the first 5–10 seconds, do not click it.

The goal is not to browse more. The goal is to preserve attention and only open the jobs that are likely to matter.

## Daily workflow
1. Search one lane only.
2. Apply the blacklist.
3. Open only the top results that pass the checklist.
4. Save strong fits to your tracker.
5. Ignore the noise.

## Best use
Use this as the default screening filter for LinkedIn job pages. It is designed for a paid LinkedIn account and for someone who wants lower noise, fewer useless clicks, and a shorter path to real opportunities.
