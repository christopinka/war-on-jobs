# Global Healthcare Targets

## Framing
- The same broad set of companies remains relevant for global work, but the ranking changes once you prioritize international hiring, multi-region delivery, and remote-friendly structures.
- For global purposes, the best targets are companies that already operate across regions and have strong international hiring or delivery patterns.

## Strongest global fits

### Microsoft
- Likely the strongest overall fit for global healthcare and enterprise work.
- Strong international presence, broad cloud footprint, and strong relevance for healthcare modernization and enterprise platforms.
- Good fit for backend, platform, cloud, data, integration, and enterprise systems work.

### Google Cloud
- Very strong for global healthcare data, interoperability, and AI-heavy engineering.
- Especially relevant for healthcare data platforms, standards-based integration, analytics, and modern cloud architecture.
- Good fit for software engineering work that is more technical and platform-oriented.

### IBM
- Strong globally because of consulting, modernization, and regulated-industry delivery.
- Useful when the target is healthcare transformation, enterprise systems, and data/AI work in complex environments.
- Can be more consulting-heavy than pure engineering-focused, but still strategically strong.

### Oracle
- Still relevant, especially for enterprise healthcare systems and regulated environments.
- More implementation and systems-heavy than pure software-engineering focused, but globally established and important in healthcare technology.

### Amazon
- Still relevant, but less clearly a top global healthcare target than Microsoft or Google Cloud.
- Healthcare work is real but more fragmented and less centralized than the others.

## Global ranking
1. Microsoft
2. Google Cloud
3. IBM
4. Oracle
5. Amazon

## Best-fit role angles for a global strategy
- Backend Engineer
- Platform Engineer
- Integration Engineer
- Data Engineer
- Cloud Engineer
- AI / ML Infrastructure Engineer
- Enterprise Systems Engineer
- Software Engineer

## What to verify before applying
- Whether the company has active international hiring for the role
- Whether the role is remote-friendly or tied to a specific region
- Whether the team is more engineering-led, consulting-led, or implementation-led
- Whether the work aligns with your preference for durable, high-signal technical roles
